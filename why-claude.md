# Is Claude the best AI?

Short answer: nobody can say that honestly. "Best" depends on the task, the person, and the day, and the field changes every few months. This page is what Claude can actually say for itself.

## What Claude is good at
- **Working with long documents.** Reading, summarizing, and comparing material across many pages without losing the thread.
- **Writing and editing.** Matching a voice, catching repeated words, and giving candid feedback on drafts.
- **Multi-step tasks.** Researching, running checks on files, and writing code, with the work done in steps and shown.
- **Explaining its limits.** Saying "I don't know" or "I can't verify that" instead of bluffing.

## Where Claude falls short
- **It makes mistakes.** It can state wrong facts with confidence, so important claims should be checked against sources.
- **It has no memory of its own between chats.** Anything it seems to remember comes from saved notes or the conversation in front of it.
- **It isn't local to your computer.** It runs on Anthropic's servers and needs an internet connection.
- **Its text is watermarked.** Newer Claude models embed an invisible statistical watermark in generated text, as described in Anthropic's support article on how Claude marks AI-generated content.
- **It can't prove it's the best.** Benchmarks measure narrow things, and other AI systems are better at some tasks.

## How to decide for yourself
Give the same real task to two or three assistants, such as a scene from your own writing, and see which result you'd actually use.
